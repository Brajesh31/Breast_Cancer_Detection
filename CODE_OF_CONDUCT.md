# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We aim to keep discussions and contributions respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, reviews, and other project spaces.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Critique code, methods, and claims without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information.
- Follow the repository's contribution, security-reporting, and data-provenance guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and disruptive conduct that prevents others from participating are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation in response to unacceptable behavior. Responses should be proportionate and aimed at keeping collaboration safe and useful.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub [@brajesh31](https://github.com/brajesh31). Avoid including sensitive personal information in public issues.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related spaces, as well as when someone is representing this project in public. It supports respectful collaboration and does not prohibit good-faith technical disagreement.
