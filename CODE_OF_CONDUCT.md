# 🤝 Community Code of Conduct

## 🌱 Our commitment

The Breast Cancer Detection repository is an educational software and data-science project. Its community should be welcoming to people across experience levels, educational backgrounds, identities, and abilities. We want discussions that are technically rigorous, honest about evidence, and respectful of the people participating.

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, project-related events, and public communication when someone is representing this project.

## 💚 Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

1. **Be respectful.** Use inclusive language and treat others with patience and consideration.
2. **Be constructive.** Explain technical criticism with evidence, reproducible examples, and actionable suggestions.
3. **Assume good faith, while checking facts.** Ask clarifying questions before attributing intent, but do not accept unsupported technical or medical claims as facts.
4. **Respect different experience levels.** Help newcomers understand project conventions without belittling them.
5. **Protect privacy.** Never disclose private contact information, credentials, patient information, or personal data.
6. **Respect attribution and licensing.** Credit third-party datasets, code, papers, images, and tools according to their terms.
7. **Keep scientific claims honest.** Distinguish experiments, hypotheses, observed results, and validated conclusions. Never present this repository as a clinically validated diagnostic system.
8. **Accept review professionally.** Respond to feedback about code, methodology, data leakage, safety, or documentation without personal attacks.
9. **Keep project spaces useful.** Stay on topic, avoid spam, and make discussions accessible to readers who encounter them later.

## 🚫 Unacceptable behavior

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained personal attacks.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted sexual attention.
- Doxxing or publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate humiliation, insults, or repeated hostile disruption.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Posting credentials, private health records, or other restricted information.
- Deliberately fabricating benchmark results, clinical claims, citations, or experiment evidence.
- Misrepresenting another contributor's work or violating licenses and attribution requirements.
- Using project spaces to promote harmful or deceptive medical advice.

Technical disagreement, strong criticism of a method, and requests for evidence are not misconduct when communicated respectfully.

## 🧭 Maintainer responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying project expectations, reviewing reports, and taking proportionate action. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include:

- A private or public request to stop the behavior.
- Editing or removing content that violates this policy.
- Closing an issue, discussion, or pull request.
- Temporarily restricting participation in a project space.
- Blocking a participant from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

The maintainer will aim to protect affected people, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and apply this policy consistently. The project is independently maintained and does not promise a formal appeal process or response-time SLA.

## 📣 Reporting a concern

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub @brajesh31](https://github.com/brajesh31). Do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue. If the report concerns a security vulnerability, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

A useful report may include:
- A short description of the behavior.
- The project space and approximate date.
- Links or other context, if safe to share.
- Whether you want the maintainer to avoid publicly identifying you.

Do not include private health information, credentials, or unrelated personal details.

## 🔐 Confidentiality and protection from retaliation

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Information will be shared only when needed to investigate and respond. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is unacceptable.

## 🌍 Scope and interpretation

This policy applies in repository-managed spaces and project-related interactions where a participant represents the project. The maintainer may clarify expectations when a situation is not explicitly listed. This document is a community standard, not legal advice or a guarantee of a particular enforcement outcome.

## 📚 Attribution

This policy is written for this repository and is informed by common open-source community standards. For questions, contact the maintainer through the repository's private communication route.
