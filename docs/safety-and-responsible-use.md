# 🩺 Safety, Responsible Use and Medical Boundary

## 🚨 Not for clinical use

This project is an educational notebook exercise. It is not a medical device, diagnostic system, screening tool, treatment recommendation engine, or clinical decision support system.

**Do not use this repository or its outputs to diagnose, rule out disease, determine treatment, triage a patient, or reassure an individual.**

## Why this boundary matters

- The existing model examples are not valid diagnosis classification evaluations.
- A public benchmark is not representative evidence for every patient, hospital, device, or population.
- False negatives and false positives can cause serious harm in medical contexts.
- Performance measured on a dataset does not establish clinical utility or safe deployment.
- No clinical validation, prospective evaluation, regulatory assessment, or quality management system is provided here.

## Responsible educational use

- Use only the included public educational data or other datasets with verified rights.
- Do not upload or commit patient records, identifiable scans, or private medical information.
- Do not present notebook output as an individual risk estimate.
- Explain the dataset's origin, limitations, and class distribution.
- Clearly label experimental results and unverified assumptions.
- Do not create screenshots or charts that imply clinical approval or patient benefit.

## Requirements for any future clinical proposal

A clinical-facing project would require a separate program covering intended use, clinical evidence, independent external validation, representative cohorts, risk management, human factors, privacy, security, legal and regulatory review, governance, monitoring, and post-deployment controls. This repository does not provide those controls.

## Reporting unsafe claims

If a contribution introduces unsupported diagnostic claims, raise a review comment or issue. For vulnerabilities or exposed sensitive data, follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) and use a private channel.
